About Us

📍 India

" The sky is not a ceiling —
it's a canvas.

Aayan

Visual Artist & Sky Dreamer

Greetings from the heart of India 🇮🇳 — I'm Aayan, a creator fascinated by the space between earth and sky. Growing up watching the monsoon clouds roll over the Deccan plateau, I found my muse in the ever-changing canvas overhead.

AVS (Art, Vision & Sky) is my creative universe — a place where I blend digital art, atmospheric photography, and storytelling to capture the moods of the world above us. From golden sunrise over the Ganges to the star-dusted nights of the Thar Desert, every sky holds a story.
